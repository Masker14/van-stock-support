Common questions

Which labels can I use?
The app prints its own numbered QR labels on A4 sheets of 21 (3 across, 7 down, 63.5 × 38.1 mm). Weatherproof vinyl sheets last best in a van. It also reads ready-made numbered barcode labels (Code 128 or QR), the barcodes already on product packaging, and NTAG213 or NTAG215 NFC stickers.

My printed labels don't line up with the stickers.
Print at 100% or "Actual size". "Fit to page" shrinks the sheet and moves the labels off the stickers.

I bought a second pack of numbered labels and the numbers repeat.
The app never links one label to two things. Scanning a label that's already in use opens what it's on. Buy the next range of numbers instead (for example 1001–2000), or use the app's own labels, which can't clash.

What does a box label do?
A label can mark one item, or a box, tray or shelf of mixed items. Scanning a box label lists what's in it, most used first, and you tap the item you took.

Why is the count wrong?
Every change is saved with the time it was made. Tap Undo straight after a mistake, or use Set count to put the right number in at any time.

Which iPhones can scan?
Scanning labels with the camera needs an iPhone XS or later. On older iPhones you can still add and count items by hand. Reading NFC tags needs an iPhone 7 or later.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Van Stock Tracker. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, or before the end of the free trial, to avoid the next charge. After you cancel you keep every item you already have: scanning, counting, the shopping list, search and export all still work. While you have more than the free 20 items, adding, renaming or relabelling items needs a subscription.
