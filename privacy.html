Privacy Policy

Van Stock Tracker for iPhone. Last updated 18 November 2026.

In short: Van Stock Tracker collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share a list, a label sheet or a CSV export yourself, and then it goes only where you send it.

What the app stores, and where

Your items, boxes, label codes, stock changes and settings are stored on your iPhone only. There is no login, no server and no cloud copy run by us. Deleting the app deletes them, so export a CSV copy if you want a backup.

Permissions

Camera: used only while the scanner is open, to read labels and barcodes. No pictures or video are recorded or uploaded.

NFC: used only when you tap a tag, to read it or to write this site's address onto a blank tag.

The app does not use your location, photos, contacts or microphone.

Sharing

When you share a shopping list, a label sheet or a CSV file, the app hands it to the iPhone's share sheet and you choose where it goes (email, messages, files, print). It is sent by the app or service you choose, not by us, and we never receive a copy.

NFC tag pages

A tag written by the app holds a web address on this site. If a tag is tapped outside the app, the phone opens a plain page here. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages; we run no analytics on it and receive no visitor data.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sold or shared, because we receive none.

Children

Van Stock Tracker is a work tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact
